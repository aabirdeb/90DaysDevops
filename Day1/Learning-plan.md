# 90-Day DevOps Learning & Career Plan

### Current Level

I am a **working professional in DevOps/SRE with around 4 years of experience**. I have hands-on exposure to AWS, Kubernetes/EKS, Terraform, CI/CD, Docker, monitoring, and production support. My focus for the next 90 days is to strengthen my practical troubleshooting and system-design skills and prepare for **DevOps/SRE roles in product-based companies**.

### 3 Goals for the Next 90 Days

**1. Build Multiple Production-Grade DevOps Projects**
Build 2–3 end-to-end projects covering application containerization, AWS infrastructure, Kubernetes deployment, CI/CD, monitoring, security, and Infrastructure as Code. Publish the projects on GitHub with proper documentation and architecture diagrams.

**2. Prepare for Product-Based Company Interviews**
Develop strong scenario-based knowledge of Kubernetes, AWS, Terraform, Linux, networking, CI/CD, and SRE concepts. Practice real production scenarios such as failed deployments, Kubernetes outages, Terraform drift, networking issues, high CPU/memory, and incident troubleshooting.

**3. Become Stronger in Kubernetes & DevOps Troubleshooting**
Go beyond commands and understand how systems work internally. Practice debugging pods, services, ingress, DNS, networking, storage, EKS, CI/CD failures, and production incidents.

### 3 Core DevOps Skills to Build

**1. Kubernetes & Linux Troubleshooting** — EKS/Kubernetes debugging, Linux administration, networking, performance issues, logs, processes, DNS, CPU, memory, and storage troubleshooting.

**2. Infrastructure & Automation** — Terraform, AWS, reusable modules, VPC, IAM, EKS, load balancers, Bash/Python automation, and GitOps concepts.

**3. CI/CD & Observability** — Build production-style pipelines using GitHub Actions/Jenkins/Azure DevOps and strengthen Prometheus, Grafana, OpenTelemetry, CloudWatch, logging, alerting, and incident response.

### Weekly Time Budget

I will dedicate approximately **2 hours per day**, around **14 hours per week**.

* **Weekdays:** 1 hour hands-on/project work + 1 hour interview preparation and concepts.
* **Weekends:** Focus on completing projects, troubleshooting labs, GitHub documentation, and mock interview scenarios.

### 90-Day Outcome

By the end of 90 days, I aim to have **2–3 strong DevOps projects on GitHub**, improved production troubleshooting skills, stronger Kubernetes/AWS/Terraform knowledge, and enough structured interview preparation to confidently target **DevOps/SRE positions at product-based companies**.
